## Full Description (Google Play / App Store — ≤4000 chars)

IdleWright is a free idle/incremental RPG where you master over 20 skills,
battle monsters and bosses, complete quests, and build your character — all
at your own pace.

Set in a medieval fantasy world, IdleWright lets you train everything from
Woodcutting and Mining to Magic and Archaeology. Actions run automatically
once started, so you can check in whenever you like and watch your character
grow stronger over time.

**Skills**
Train 20+ skills across gathering, crafting, combat, and support:
- Gather resources: Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Farming, Hunter
- Craft and upgrade gear: Smithing, Crafting, Fletching, Herblore
- Master the arcane: Magic, Divination, Runecrafting, Prayer
- Fight and explore: Combat, Slayer, Dungeoneering, Thieving, Agility

**Combat**
Fight through dozens of monsters and unlock powerful bosses. Equip melee,
ranged, or magic gear and take on increasingly powerful foes, including
the Void Dragon, Leviathan Kings, and the Ancient Harbinger.

**Quests**
Complete quests like Goblin Diplomacy, Discovering the Lost City, and
Echoes of the Deep to unlock new areas, bosses, and rewards.

**Collect Everything**
Track your progress with a full Collection Log, Achievement system, and
Slayer task tracker.

**Free Forever**
IdleWright is completely free — no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts required.
