IdleWright — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-05

IdleWright ("the App") is a free idle RPG game developed by UselessTechSupport. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not require an account, does not contact any server, and does not transmit any information about you or your device to us or any third party.

Save Data

Your game progress is saved locally on your device using the device's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Advertising & Analytics

The App contains no advertisements and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party SDKs, services, or libraries that collect data.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, there are no special considerations for users under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions? Contact us via the support link on the app store listing.
